Kernel: Suvkernel 3.2 — battery and deep sleep
- Real deep sleep: the SoC (AOSS, CX rail, DDR) now powers down with the
  screen off. On deep suspend it never did; the kernel now uses s2idle,
  which cut idle power a lot
- RAM: anonymous memory faults in 4K pages again (64K folios caused
  swap churn on 6 GB); zram runs lz4 for the swap path and recompresses
  idle pages with zstd, so swap-in/out is faster for the same packing
- lmkd no longer kills the launcher and apps under a memory burst
- Wakeup sources the suspend HAL reads are labelled, so their stats and
  CPU time are accounted
- Carried over: NFC, Wi-Fi and modem crash recovery, and the Linux
  6.12.98-116 storage, UFS, dm-verity and PM fixes
Display
- 90 Hz refresh rate added, next to 60 and 120 Hz
Performance and battery
- Lighter scroll and animation boosts: the CPU no longer jumps to its top
  clocks on every scroll, and the cores can sleep between frames
- The scheduler reacts to load in 12 ms instead of 20 ms, so clocks go up
  faster when needed and come back down sooner
- Audio battery listener turned off
Camera
- Photo and video filters work on the rear and front cameras
Telephony
- Stop logging the satellite config lookup (~300 lines a minute)
