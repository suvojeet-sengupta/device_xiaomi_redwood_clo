Battery
- Smart CPU limits: light work and short bursts no longer push the CPU
  to its top speeds, which cost the most power. Games, benchmarks and
  app launches still get full speed
- The faster 12 ms scheduler setting now stays on (the perf HAL used to
  reset it)
Charging
- Faster charging while you use the phone: the thermal profile now
  matches FKM's "Dynamic (evaluation)", so charging isn't held at 1.2 A
  once the phone gets warm
