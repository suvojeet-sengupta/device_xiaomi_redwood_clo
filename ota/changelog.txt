New in this build
- Smart CPU limits: light work and short bursts no longer push the CPU
  to its top speeds, which cost the most power. Games, benchmarks and
  app launches still get full speed
- Faster charging while you use the phone: the thermal profile now
  matches FKM's "Dynamic (evaluation)", so charging isn't held at 1.2 A
  once the phone gets warm
- The faster 12 ms scheduler setting now stays on (the perf HAL used to
  reset it)
Carried over from 20261108
- 90 Hz refresh rate, next to 60 and 120 Hz
- Lighter scroll and animation boosts, cores sleep between frames
- Real deep sleep (s2idle): the SoC powers down with the screen off
- zram lz4 with zstd recompression, lmkd no longer kills the launcher
  under a memory burst
- Photo and video filters work on the rear and front cameras
- Audio battery listener off, satellite config log spam removed
