Kernel: Suvkernel 6.18 (6.18.33, Android Common Kernel android17-6.18)

Coming from a 6.12 build: flash this once from recovery. From the
20261112 6.18 build it comes over the air.

Battery and deep sleep
- The phone reaches deep sleep again with the screen off: the CPU
  cluster power domain, its suppliers and the bus votes now drop
- IPA (mobile data) no longer keeps the phone awake: its interrupt is
  level triggered and a stuck TAG command no longer blocks clock gating
- Drivers that never probe release their suppliers after 30 seconds,
  so their power domains can turn off

Display
- New 100 Hz and 110 Hz refresh rates, in the Refresh Rate quick tile
  and in Refresh rate per-app
- Apps that already had 60, 90 or 120 Hz per-app keep their setting
